Founders Invitational

An 18-hole solo matchplay event

All closest to pin tiebreakers are best of 5 and will be played on the same hole a match ends if possible. Find a spot off green about 30 paces from the hole towards the next tee box.


Front 9 - Group Stage

Players are divided into pots of 3 (or 4 in some cases) based on last year's standings

One player from each pot will be drawn into each group

Pot 1: Top 3 players from last year's standings

Pot 2: Next 3 (or 4) players from last year's standings

Pot 3: Last 3 players from last year's standings


Group Matches

Once groups are formed, players will compete in group matches within their group

The first 3 holes is 1 match, the next 3 holes is another match, and the last 3 holes is a third match

Since most groups will have 3 players, each player will play 2 matches in the group stage, order will be assinged randomly via draw

When a group member is not playing a match, they will still play the hole, as strokes on the front 9 will be used as a tiebreaker

Matches have 3 outcomes: win, loss, or tie

Win: 3 points, Tie: 1 point, Loss: 0 points

Winning your group or placing 2nd will advance you to the playoff stage on the back 9


Group Tiebreakers

If players in the same group are tied on points, the following tiebreakers will be used in order:

1. Head-to-head record within the group

2. Total strokes on the front 9

3. A best of 5 closest to the pin


Back 9 - Playoff Stage

Players who finish top 2 in their group advance to the playoff stage on the back 9

Players who finished last in their group will play for 7th-9th place on the back 9


Playoff Seeding

Players will be seeded in the playoff bracket based on their performance in the group stage

Group winners will be seeded 1-3 based on points, then strokes on the front 9, then a closest to the pin tiebreaker if needed

The top 2 seeds will receive a bye to the semifinals, played on holes 13-15 (they will still play holes 10-12, but those holes will not count for the playoff stage)

The 3rd seed will be the remaining group winner, with seeds 4-6 being the group runners up, seeded based on points, then strokes on the front 9, then a closest to the pin tiebreaker


First Group to Tee Off - 7th through 9th Place

The first group to tee off will be the 7th-9th place group, they will play holes 10-18 in a similar format to the group stage, with 3 matches of 3 holes each, and points awarded for wins and ties

Holes 10-12: 8 seed vs 9 seed, 7 seed plays for stroke tiebreaker

Holes 13-15: 7 seed vs 8 seed, 9 seed plays for stroke tiebreaker

Holes 16-18: 7 seed vs 9 seed, 8 seed plays for stroke tiebreaker

Placement in the 7th-9th place group will be determined by points, then strokes on holes 10-18, then a closest to the pin tiebreaker if needed


Second Group to Tee Off - First Round of Playoffs

The second group to tee off will consist of a 4 man group of the 3rd-6th seeds

They will play holes 10-12 in a head to head matchplay format:

3rd seed vs 6th seed, 4th seed vs 5th seed

Since only 3 holes are played, if a match is tied after 3 holes, the tiebreaker will be total strokes on holes 10-12, then a closest to the pin tiebreaker if needed

Winners of the first round matches wait on the 13th tee box for the 1st and 2nd seeds to catch up

Losers of the first round matches will go ahead and play for 5th and 6th place on holes 13-15. If tied after 3 holes, the tiebreaker will be total strokes on holes 13-15, then a closest to the pin tiebreaker if needed. Holes 16-18 can be played but will not count towards playoff results


Third Group to Tee Off - Semifinals and Finals

The final group to tee off will consist of the 1st and 2nd seeds

They will play holes 10-12 as warmup, but those holes will not count towards playoff results

They will meet the winners of the first round matches on hole 13 and will play the semifinals on holes 13-15

Seed 1 will play the lower remaining seed, while seed 2 will play the higher remaining seed

Winners of the semifinals will play in the finals on holes 16-18, while the losers will play for 3rd and 4th place on holes 16-18

Since only 3 holes are played in the semifinals and finals, if a match is tied after 3 holes, the tiebreaker will be total strokes on holes 13-15 for the semifinals and holes 16-18 for the finals, then a closest to the pin tiebreaker if needed
